# Fernhill Supply Co. returns, refunds and exceptions policy (v3.2)

1. Standard returns. Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Original shipping is not refunded.
2. Summit members. Customers with a Summit membership get 60 days instead of 30 for standard returns and used-item returns.
3. Final sale. Items marked clearance are final sale and cannot be returned, except under the defect warranty (section 5).
4. Used items. Items that have been used outdoors can be returned within the return window for a 50% refund of the item price, unless they are defective.
5. Defect warranty. Items that fail because of a manufacturing defect within 1 year of delivery get a full refund of the item price, whether or not they were used or on clearance. Damage from misuse (fire, cuts, animals, improper washing) is not a defect.
6. Shipping damage. Items that arrive damaged get a full refund, including original shipping, if the customer reports it within 7 days of delivery. After 7 days, handle it as a standard return.
7. Refund method. Refunds go to the original payment method. Store credit at 110% of the refund amount may be offered instead.
8. Approval limits. Support agents may issue refunds up to $250 per order. Anything above $250 must be escalated to a human supervisor.
9. Exceptions. Exceptions to this policy can be approved only for documented safety issues or carrier loss confirmed by tracking. Approved exceptions are refunded in full, including original shipping. All other exception requests are denied.
10. Repeat refunds. Customers with 3 or more refunds in the past 90 days must be escalated to a human supervisor before any refund. Supervisors approve the refund if the request is otherwise within policy and every one of those prior refunds was for a defect or shipping damage; otherwise they deny it.
